Acceptable use

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Use this service only for public GitHub repository security review. Reports are informational coverage records, not a guarantee that a repository is secure and not a replacement for a professional assessment.

Do not disrupt availability, evade rate or quota controls, scan private or unlawfully obtained data, probe the service without authorization, or deliberately place personal data, credentials, or exploit payloads into the report workflow. A report is not permission to exploit a finding. Disclose issues responsibly to the repository owner.

The service may refuse, delay, or remove requests to protect its zero-cost capacity, users, and upstream services. Honest partial, failed, and unsupported outcomes are part of the product contract.
